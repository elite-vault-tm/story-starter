Step One

Tell Your Story

Start with one real experience.

It might be something that taught you a lesson, changed your mind, made you laugh, frustrated you, surprised you, or reminded you why your work matters.

You're going to use that experience as the starting point for your content.

Here's what to do

  1. Copy the prompt below.
  2. Open ChatGPT, Claude, or the AI tool you already use.
  3. Paste the prompt into the chat.
  4. Use the microphone if you would rather talk than type.
  5. Tell your story in your own words.

Your job is to tell the story. AI's job is to help you find the message inside it.

The Prompt

I want to create content from a real experience I've had. I'm going to tell you the story in my own words. It may be messy or out of order. That's fine. Listen for the parts that matter most. Here's the experience: [Tell your story here] From what I shared, help me identify: the main lesson the part my audience may relate to the natural connection to what I do, teach, sell, offer, or believe Don't write the content yet. First, reflect back the strongest message you hear in simple language. If something important is missing, ask me only the clarifying questions you need. Do not invent details, emotions, or outcomes I did not share.

Now paste it into your AI tool and tell your story.

Tip If talking feels easier than typing, use the microphone. Talk naturally. Pause. Change direction. Correct yourself. Tell the story the way you would tell it to a friend. AI can still follow the conversation.
Your story can be personal without sharing private details Share the part that supports the message. Keep the rest for yourself.
Between the two

Find the Message

Once you share the experience, AI will help you pull out what you learned, where your audience may connect, and how the story relates to what you do, offer, teach, or believe.

Read what it gives you. If something does not sound right, tell AI.

You can say "That's not quite what I meant" or "That doesn't sound like me. Let's try that again."

Step Two

Choose Your Content Angle

Now you have the story and the message.

The next step is deciding how you want to use it.

This second prompt will take what AI already learned from your story and give you three different ways you could turn it into content.

Here's what to do

  1. Stay in the same AI conversation.
  2. Copy the second prompt below.
  3. Paste it directly underneath the conversation you just had.
  4. AI will use the story and message you already shared.
  5. Review the three content angles and choose the one that feels most like you.
Stay in the same conversation You do not have to start a new chat. Keep working in the same conversation so AI already has the context from your story.

The Prompt

Based on the story and message we identified, give me 3 different content angles I could take from this experience. For each angle, give me: a hook the main message a simple CTA or conversation-starting question an optional format suggestion, such as a short video, caption, carousel, email, or story post Make the three angles meaningfully different so I can choose the one that feels most natural to me. Keep everything rooted in my real experience. Do not add details, emotions, struggles, or results I did not give you. Do not write the full post unless I ask.

Paste this into the same AI conversation.

Read all three. Pick the direction that sounds most like something you would really say. Then continue the conversation with AI. "I like option 2. Let's build that one out."
